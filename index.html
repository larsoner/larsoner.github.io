Short Bio

I am a principal research scientist at the University of Washington Institute for Learning and Brain Sciences, working primarily with human electrophysiological data: magnetoencephalography (MEG), electroencephalography (EEG), functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), and intracranial EEG (iEEG).

I am a member of the MNE-Python Steering Council and a maintainer of SciPy and other packages in the scientific Python ecosystem. My work on keeping these tools findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable (FAIR) is supported by an NIH NINDS Research Specialist Award (R50NS147973), along with other national grants.

Software

MNE-Python Steering Council Complete M/EEG analysis: preprocessing, forward and inverse modeling, connectivity, statistics, and machine learning. SciPy Maintainer Core scientific Python library, including the signal processing routines used across the ecosystem. PyVistaQt Maintainer Qt integration for PyVista, enabling interactive 3D visualization in desktop applications. sphinx-gallery Maintainer Builds rich HTML example galleries from Python scripts for project documentation.
